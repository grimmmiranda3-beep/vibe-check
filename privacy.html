Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Vibe Check is designed to let people share an anonymous feeling about a local place without creating an account.

Information we collect

When you submit a Vibe Check, the selected vibe and the place identifier are used to maintain aggregate check-in counts. The app does not require your name, email address, phone number, or an account to submit a vibe.

Local storage

The website may use browser storage to support the experience. Clearing your browser storage can remove locally stored app data.

Location and places

Vibe Check can search for local businesses using Google Places data. Search requests and place information are used to return business results, ratings, opening status, photos, and links to Google Maps.

Third parties

Google services may provide place information and review-related content displayed by Vibe Check. Their own terms and privacy policies apply to their services.

Data retention

Aggregate vibe counts may be stored to show community activity for a place. Vibe Check does not intentionally attach a submitted vibe to a public user profile.

Contact

If you need to contact the Vibe Check team about privacy, use the contact method provided on the Vibe Check website or business materials.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Vibe Check develops. The latest version will be posted on this page.
