# Vibe Check — 2027 Launch Roadmap

## Product North Star
Make Vibe Check the fastest way to answer: **"What's the vibe there right now?"**

## Phase 1 — Launch foundation
- Anonymous check-ins
- Positive + Not My Vibe feedback
- Live community score
- Trending places
- Shareable place/vibe cards
- Strong first-run experience
- Abuse/rate-limit protection
- Privacy-safe analytics

## Phase 2 — Retention
- Daily/weekly vibe streaks
- Badges for exploring and contributing
- Personal vibe history (without requiring public identity)
- Vibe alerts for saved places
- "Popular right now" and "changing fast" discovery

## Phase 3 — Social growth
- Share a live vibe card
- Deep links back to a place
- Friend/group planning without exposing check-in identity
- City-level vibe leaderboards
- Time-based events and vibe moments

## Phase 4 — Business revenue
- Claimed business profiles
- Business analytics dashboard
- Trend alerts
- Sponsored placement clearly labeled
- Premium business subscriptions

## Phase 5 — Defensibility
- Vibe history by hour/day
- Better personalization
- Anti-spam/reputation systems
- City launch playbook
- Public API/partner opportunities

## Product principle
Do not add features just because they are possible. Every feature should make Vibe Check more useful, more shareable, more trustworthy, or more valuable to businesses.
